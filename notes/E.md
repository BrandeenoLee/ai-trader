# Strategy E: Claude's pick: Large-cap momentum, fully invested, low turnover

## Approach
Hold 2-4 liquid large-cap momentum leaders plus a QQQ core, with cash at 10% or less. Cash drag cost us before; being fully invested worked this week (-1.54% vs SPY -1.96%). Edge comes from tilting toward leaders while staying invested, not from timing.

## Universe
- US stocks over $10B market cap with average daily volume over $50M, or liquid ETFs (QQQ, XLK, SMH, XLF, XLI).
- Skip anything up more than 15% in 3 days.

## Entry
- Price above the 50-day average, and the 50-day above the 200-day.
- Limits: last price to +0.3% above. Never bid below the last price. After two expiries, drop the name and use QQQ instead.
- Don't open a position within 2 days of earnings.
- If SPY closes below its 50-day: open no new single stocks and hold at most 2. Keep the QQQ core.

## Sizing
- Confidence 8 or higher: 25-33%. Confidence 6-7: about 20%. Confidence 5 or lower: skip.
- Cash sweep: any cash above 10% goes into QQQ at last +0.2%, at the same check-in.
- No more than 2 single stocks per sector (ETFs don't count).
- Confidence is NOT predictive. Tournament results: 1-4 avg +0.70%, 5-7 avg -4.68%, small sample. Use it only for sizing. Never raise size because a story feels strong.

## Exits (judged on the prior session's CLOSE, not intraday prices)
- Single stocks: sell on a close 8% below entry, or 2 consecutive closes more than 2% below the 50-day average.
- QQQ core: no 50-day exit (it is the beta anchor, and selling it recreates cash drag). Sell it only to fund a new leader, or if QQQ closes 10% below entry.
- Money from a stock exit goes into QQQ at the same check-in unless a qualifying leader exists.
- Rotate at most 1 slot per week. Trim half if a position is up 20% or more within 2 weeks.
- Ignore red days and headlines.

## Habits
- Holdings: META about 32%, MSFT about 20%, QQQ about 37%, cash about 11%. Default action: hold.
- Check the exit rules each check-in using closing prices. Expect 0-3 trades per week.
- Log a confidence score and a one-line reason for every trade.
