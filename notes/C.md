# Strategy C notes: Catalyst reader

## Identity
Trade only when a specific headline explains a price move, or explains why a stock didn't move. Look for over- and under-reactions. Holding cash is allowed. It lagged SPY in a rising week (W1) and helped in a falling week (W2).

## Entry rules
- Each session, name the single best catalyst setup and give it a verdict: (a) what the news changes over the next 12 months, (b) whether the move is bigger than that, (c) buy, or hold with a specific reason.
- **Overreaction (>5% move on speculative or narrative news):** wait for proof the selling has slowed before buying. That means a green close, or a session that holds above the prior low. Price down several days in a row is not enough by itself. W2: SMH was bought mid-slide and kept falling.
- **Underreaction:** concrete material news with less than a 1% move.
- **Sector or macro selloffs** (CPI, rates, credit fears) are not single-headline catalysts. Positive company news doesn't cancel macro selling, so treat these setups as lower quality.
- Don't buy into a fresh structural downtrend or real fundamental damage.

## Data hygiene
- If every price shows 0.0%, treat the data as stale and hold. Say so.

## Sizing and confidence
- Starters are confidence 5–6 and small. Add only after confirmation.
- Tournament data: confidence 1–4 avg +0.70% (n=3), 5–7 avg −4.68% (n=7), no 8+ trades. Mid confidence hasn't paid off. Keep sizes small and don't use 8+.
- Hold at most 2 positions.

## Exits (enforce these)
- **Time stop:** if there's no bounce within 3 sessions of the fill, exit. Don't keep writing "hold, not adding".
- **Thesis break:** if new negative news widens the story (e.g. CoreWeave credit stress, AI-financing fears on top of CPI), the move may be justified. Exit or cut by half.
- Take profit once about half the overreaction has retraced.
- SMH (filled 10/15 @ 565.54): the time stop is the next session unless it closed green.
