## Entry setup (core)
- Look for liquid large caps with a sharp 5-day drop of about 2x their normal 5-day move, 60-day trend intact, and price near or above its 50d MA.
- **Relative-weakness dips count too.** In a rising market, absolute dips are rare. A name that is down at least 3% over 1-5 days while SPY is flat or up, with its trend intact, qualifies.
- Skip drops caused by confirmed bad news (earnings miss, guidance cut, sector shock). "Looks stock-specific" is not enough to skip. Check the news, and if nothing clear turns up, the dip is tradeable.
- Make at most one new entry per day.

## Deploying cash
- Target 2-4 positions and 40-60% of the account invested, spread across sectors (financials, healthcare, staples, industrials, tech).
- **Anti-idle rule:** if less than 25% is invested and there has been no entry for 3 straight decision days, buy the best qualifying candidate (even a marginal relative-weakness dip) at conf 3, about $70-90.
- If SPY makes a new -3% or worse day, pause new buys. Keep the existing positions unless their stops are hit.

## Costs and orders
- Spread penalties total $5.29 (about 1.06% of the account). Trade only large, tight-spread names, with each position at least about $70.
- Fills come in 1-3% worse than the limit. Set the limit at or slightly below the last price. Re-base the stop and target on the actual fill. Skip a trade if less than about 3% of bounce is left from a realistic fill.

## Exits (mechanical)
- Defaults from the fill: take profit +5%, stop -5%, time stop 10 trading days. Sell on the time-stop date (NVDA lesson).
- Exit on a close at or below the stop, within about 1% of it on a down-market day, or right away on bad news.
