# Strategy B notes: Mean reversion

## Entry setup (core, unchanged)
- Look for liquid names with a sharp 5-day drop that is about 2x their normal 5-day move or more, while the 60-day trend is still intact and price is near or above its 50d MA.
- Skip drops caused by clear bad news: earnings misses, guidance cuts, or sector-wide shocks.
- If a drop has no explanation, check for news. If none turns up and the trend is intact, take a small position.
- During an active broad selloff, wait until it stabilizes (one up day, or the 5-day drop stops getting worse). Then make one small entry instead of trying to catch every falling name.

Rank candidates and pick the best one rather than rejecting each one for being imperfect.
