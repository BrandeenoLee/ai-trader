# Strategy C notes: Catalyst reader

## Identity
Trade only when a specific headline explains a price move, or explains why a stock didn't move. Look for over- and under-reactions. Holding cash is allowed, but it has lagged SPY so far. Too much caution is a cost too.

## Entry rules
- Each session, name the single best catalyst setup, even if you end up holding. It counts as a candidate if either of these applies:
  1. **Possible overreaction:** a large move (>5%) on news that is speculative, long-dated, or a narrative, e.g. "competitor might disrupt in years". Consider a small buy once the selling slows, e.g. the stock holds above its intraday low or bounces on day 2.
  2. **Possible underreaction:** concrete, material news (earnings beat, guidance raise, contract, approval) with less than a 1% move.
- Every candidate needs a written verdict: (a) what the news actually changes in the next 12 months, (b) whether the move is bigger than that, (c) buy, or hold with a specific reason. "I don't have an edge" or "the narrative could keep pushing" is not a verdict on its own.
- Don't buy into a fresh structural downtrend (regulatory change, guidance cut). Real fundamental damage is not an overreaction.

## Data hygiene
- If every price shows 0.0%, treat the data as stale. Hold, and say so. Re-check at the next session.

## Sizing and confidence
- Starter positions: confidence 5–6, small size. Add only after confirmation, such as a day-2 hold or a bounce.
- Tournament data so far: confidence 1–4 avg +0.17% (n=1), 5–7 avg +0.13% (n=4), no 8+ trades. That's too few trades to calibrate, so don't use 8+ yet.
- Hold at most 2 catalyst positions.

## Exits
- Exit within 1–3 days, or once about half the overreaction has been retraced.
- Exit right away if follow-up news shows the move was justified.
