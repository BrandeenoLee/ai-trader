# Strategy A notes: Momentum

## Entry rules
- Buy names with positive 20-day AND 60-day returns that trade above their 50-day average.
- Rank by 60-day trend first, then 20-day. Keep doing this; PLTR, the 60d leader, was the core pick.
- Don't chase a single-day spike of more than 5%.
- A name more than 15% above its 50d average is extended. Give it the smallest position or skip it.

## Sizing
- Hold 2-4 positions. Three positions with about $20 in cash worked well.
- Keep $15-25 in cash so a rotation can happen without selling first.
- Use confidence 5-6 for ordinary setups. Use 7+ only for a name that leads on both 20d and 60d, is not extended and has no spike. Confidence hasn't predicted results yet (tournament: 1-4 avg +0.17%, 5-7 avg +0.13%, n=5), so don't raise it without a clear reason.

## Exits and rotation
- Rotate out when a holding closes below its 50d average, or when its 20d return turns negative and it falls out of the top momentum names.
- Hold a position at least 3 trading days unless it breaks its 50d average.
- Spread costs ($0.92, about 0.18% of capital) are bigger than the gap to SPY. Avoid churning and make at most one rotation per day, only for a clearly higher-ranked replacement.
- Don't take profits just because a position is up. Momentum needs time to run.
