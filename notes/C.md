# Strategy C notes: Catalyst reader

## Identity
Trade only when a specific headline explains a price move, or explains why a stock didn't move. Look for over- and under-reactions. Holding cash is allowed. Most of the lead over SPY so far comes from holding cash in falling weeks (W2–W4). That isn't catalyst skill, and it will lag in a rising week.

## Entry rules
- Each session, name the single best catalyst setup and give it a verdict: (a) what the news changes over 12 months, (b) whether the move is bigger than that, (c) buy, or hold with a specific reason.
- **Watchlist carry-forward (new):** keep a list of every overreaction you flagged but skipped as "first session" or "no green close". At the start of each session, check each name first. Did it close green, or hold above the prior low for a full session? If so, and the thesis is unchanged, take a small starter. In W4, GOOGL and INTC were flagged on day 1 and never revisited, so the confirmation rule was only ever used as a reason to wait.
- **Overreaction (>5% move on specific news):** buy only after a green close, or a full session holding above the prior low. An intraday bounce doesn't count.
- **No headline, no trade:** a big drop with no explaining headline (W4: BA) is not a catalyst setup. Skip it.
- **Underreaction:** concrete material news with less than a 1% move. Write down one candidate every session, even in selloffs. This is the main way to make money in up weeks.
- Prefer single-company catalysts. If a sector or macro selloff keeps adding new bad news, stay out.
- Don't buy into a fresh structural downtrend or real fundamental damage (NFLX W3, XYL guidance cut W4).

## Data hygiene
- If every price shows 0.0%, treat the data as stale and hold. Say so.

## Sizing and confidence
- Tournament results by stated confidence: 1–4 avg +0.75% (n=3), 5–7 avg −5.11% (n=10), 8–10 avg −3.90% (n=2). Higher confidence has not meant better results.
- Starters use confidence 3–5 and stay small. Add only after a further confirmed up close. Never use 8+.
- Hold at most 2 positions.

## Exits
- **Time stop:** if the position isn't up within 3 sessions of the fill, exit on the next decision.
- **Thesis break:** if new negative news widens the story, exit or cut by half.
- **Take profit** once about half the overreaction has retraced.
