# Strategy E: Claude's pick: Large-cap momentum, low turnover

## Approach
Hold 3-4 liquid, large-cap stocks or sector ETFs that have strong 3-6 month momentum and are trading above a rising trend. Momentum in large caps is one of the best-documented edges. Trading costs and noise are what kill small accounts, so trade rarely and with intent.

## Universe
- US stocks over $10B market cap with average daily volume over $50M, or liquid sector/thematic ETFs (e.g., XLK, SMH, XLF, XLI).
- Ignore the small-cap movers feed and penny stocks. Don't buy anything that is up more than 15% in the last 3 days (chasing).

## Entry
- Only pick from the momentum-leaders list. Price must be above its 50-day average, and the 50-day must be above the 200-day.
- Prefer an entry on a 1-3 day pullback or a flat day over buying a gap-up. Use a limit near the last price or a little under.
- Don't open new positions within 2 days of a stock's earnings report.
- If SPY is below its 50-day average, or the headlines show a broad selloff, buy at most 2 positions and keep the rest in cash.

## Sizing
- Target 3-4 positions of roughly equal size, 25-33% of equity each. Keep about 5% in cash.
- Size by confidence: 8 or higher gets the full slot, 6-7 gets about two-thirds, 5 or lower means skip.
- Hold no more than 2 positions in the same sector.

## Exits
- Stop: sell if a position closes 8% below entry, or below its 50-day average.
- Rotate out only if a name drops off the leaders list AND a clearly stronger candidate exists. Rotate at most once per week per slot.
- Trim half of a position if it's up 20% or more within 2 weeks.
- Don't sell because of one red day or one headline unless it changes the company's fundamentals (fraud, guidance cut).

## Habits
- Default action at a check-in is "do nothing." Aim for 2-6 trades per week in total.
- Log a confidence score and a one-line reason for every trade so the reviewer can calibrate.
