# Strategy B notes: Mean reversion

## Entry setup (core, unchanged)
- Look for liquid names with a sharp 5-day drop of about 2x their normal 5-day move or more, while the 60-day trend is still intact and price is near or above its 50d MA.
- Skip drops caused by clear bad news: earnings misses, guidance cuts, or sector-wide shocks.
- If a drop has no explanation, check for news. If none turns up and the trend is intact, take a small position.
- During an active broad selloff (e.g. QQQ -4% days), wait until it stabilizes: one up day, or the 5-day drop stops getting worse. Then make one small entry instead of trying to catch every falling name.

## Deploying cash
- We are 100% cash after a broad selloff. That has protected the lead (+0.28% vs SPY -2.51%), but cash can't beat SPY in a rebound.
- After the first stabilization day, scan for the best 1-2 intact-trend dips. Prefer non-tech sectors (financials, healthcare, staples, industrials, energy). Rank the candidates and buy the best one rather than rejecting each one for being imperfect.
- Target 2-4 positions from different sectors, with about 40-60% of the account invested when setups exist. Build up over several days, not all at once.

## Costs and orders
- Spread costs total $3.87 (~0.77% of the account). Trade large, tight-spread names only. Each position should be ≥ ~$70. Skip a trade if the expected bounce is under ~3%.
- Place limits near the current price. Buys have filled 1-3% worse than the limit, so size for that.

## Exits (enforce mechanically)
- At each entry, write down the exact stop price, take-profit price and time-stop date. Check all three at the start of every decision.
- Time stop: on the time-stop date, sell that day. Don't wait for the next day. NVDA passed its 10/29 time stop at about -3.6% and was held. The next day it gapped to -8.8%, and we sold at 201.33 (-$6.18). Exiting on time would have saved about $3.
- Stop: if price closes at or below the stop, or is within ~1% of it on a down-market day, exit.
- Defaults: take profit near the pre-drop level (~+5%). Cut at -5%, or right away on bad news. Exit after ~10 trading days with no bounce. Following these rules gave CRM +5.8% and TSLA +5.3%.

## Confidence
- Tournament data: conf 1-4 averaged +0.75% (3 buys), conf 5-7 averaged -5.11% (10), and conf 8-10 averaged -3.90% (2). Higher stated confidence keeps doing worse.
- Default dip buys to conf 3-4. Use 5 only for a textbook setup after stabilization with a clean news check. Never go above 5 on buys.
