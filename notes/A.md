# Strategy A notes: Momentum

## Entry rules
- Buy names with positive 20-day AND 60-day returns that trade above their 50-day average.
- Rank by 60d trend first, then 20d. PLTR is the core holding.
- Skip any name with a single-day spike of more than 5%.
- A name more than 15% above its 50d average is extended. Skip it, or give it the smallest size. Extended AMD lost 14% in a week.
- Hold at most 2 names from the high-beta tech/semis cluster. PLTR is already one, so the next buys should come from other sectors (payments, energy, industrials, health care). V, bought 8.3% above its 50d, fits this profile.

## Sizing
- Hold 2-4 positions, keeping $15-25 in cash.
- Default confidence is 5. Use 6 only for a name that leads on both 20d and 60d, is not extended and has no spike. Never use 7+. Tournament data still runs the wrong way (1-4: +2.29% n=3; 5-7: -5.48% n=8). The sample is small, but stated confidence is not predicting returns, so don't size up on conviction.

## Exits and rotation
- Hard stop: sell any position that closes 8% or more below entry.
- Early warning: log every holding's % from entry each day. If a holding is 6% or more below entry AND below its 50d average or its 20d return is negative, sell it then. Don't wait for -8%. META went from -4.3% to -10.4% in two sessions and exited past the stop (-$17.34).
- Rotate out when a holding closes below its 50d average, or when its 20d return turns negative and it drops out of the top names.
- Hold at least 3 trading days unless a stop triggers.
- Spread penalties have reached $3.13. Make at most one rotation per day, and only into a clearly higher-ranked name.
- Don't take profits just because a position is up.

## Execution
- Place limit orders near the current quote. Check every fill against its limit. This week both fills came in on the right side of their limits (V 403.68 vs 404.5, META 646.65 vs 643).
