# Strategy A notes: Momentum

## Entry rules
- Buy names with positive 20-day AND 60-day returns that trade above their 50-day average.
- Rank by 60d trend first, then 20d. No holding is "core". Every position follows the same rules. Calling PLTR core led to holding it from entry down to -7.5% (-$12.42).
- Skip any name with a single-day spike of more than 5%.
- A name more than 15% above its 50d average is extended. Skip it. Extended AMD lost 14% in a week.
- Hold at most 1 name from the high-beta tech/semis cluster. Those names have caused the biggest losses (AMD, META, PLTR).
- Prefer sector ETFs (XLE, XLV, XLI, XLF) or large low-beta leaders (V, MA). Spreads and volatility are lower, and that suits a $500 account.
- Don't buy a name that overlaps a current holding (e.g. CVX or XOM while holding XLE).

## Sizing
- Hold 2-4 positions, keeping $15-25 in cash.
- Use confidence 5 for every trade. Never go above 5. Tournament data: 1-4 averaged +0.75% (n=3), 5-7 averaged -5.11% (n=10), 8-10 averaged -3.90% (n=2). Higher conviction has not produced better returns.

## Exits and rotation
- Hard stop: sell any position that closes 8% or more below entry.
- Early warning: log every holding's % from entry each day. Sell at -6% or worse if the name is also below its 50d average, has a negative 20d return, or has dropped out of the 20d leaders. This worked on PLTR: it exited before -8%.
- Rotate out when a holding closes below its 50d average, or when its 20d return turns negative and it drops out of the top names.
- Hold at least 3 trading days unless a stop triggers.
- Spread penalties have reached $3.97, which is nearly 1% of capital. Make at most one trade per day. Only rotate into a clearly higher-ranked name.
- Don't take profits just because a position is up.

## Execution
- Place limit orders near the current quote and check every fill against its limit. The XLE fill (69.69 vs a 69.85 limit) came in on the right side.
