# Strategy C notes: Catalyst reader

## Identity
Trade only when a specific headline explains a price move, or explains why a stock didn't move. Look for over- and under-reactions. Holding cash is allowed. So far most of the edge over SPY has come from holding cash in falling weeks (W2, W3). In W1, a rising week, it lagged. That edge doesn't count as catalyst skill, and it fails in rising markets.

## Entry rules
- Each session, name the single best catalyst setup and give it a verdict: (a) what the news changes over the next 12 months, (b) whether the move is bigger than that, (c) buy, or hold with a specific reason.
- **Overreaction (>5% move on speculative or narrative news):** buy only after proof the selling has slowed. That means a green **close**, or a full session that holds above the prior low. An intraday bounce doesn't count. On 10/19 an intraday chip bounce was treated as a "green session" to justify holding.
- **Underreaction:** concrete material news with less than a 1% move. Check for these every session, not just selloffs. They are the main way to make money in up weeks.
- **Prefer single-company catalysts.** Sector or macro selloffs (AI capex, CPI, rates, credit) are lower quality. If a sector selloff keeps adding new bad news, stay out.
- Don't buy into a fresh structural downtrend or real fundamental damage (W3 example: NFLX at 2-year lows after a cut to key metrics).
- **Don't let cash become the default.** If a clean single-name setup meets the rules, take a small starter.

## Data hygiene
- If every price shows 0.0%, treat the data as stale and hold. Say so.

## Sizing and confidence
- Tournament data: confidence 1–4 avg +2.29% (n=3); 5–7 avg −5.48% (n=8); no 8+ trades. Higher stated confidence hasn't meant better results.
- Starters use confidence 4–5 and stay small. Add only after a confirmed bounce. Never use 8+.
- Hold at most 2 positions.

## Exits (working; keep enforcing)
- **Time stop:** if the position isn't up within 3 sessions of the fill, exit on the next decision. Don't stretch the deadline.
- **Thesis break:** if new negative news widens the story, exit or cut by half.
- **Take profit** once about half the overreaction has retraced.
