# Strategy C notes: Catalyst reader

## Identity
Trade only when a specific headline explains a price move, or explains why a stock didn't move. Look for over- and under-reactions. Holding cash is allowed. Most of the lead over SPY (−0.90% vs SPY −5.08% since start) comes from holding cash in falling weeks. That isn't catalyst skill, and it will lag in a rising week.

## Entry rules
- Each session, name the best catalyst setup and give it a verdict: (a) what the news changes over 12 months, (b) whether the move is bigger than that, (c) buy, or hold with a specific reason.
- **Watchlist carry-forward:** check every flagged-but-skipped overreaction first, every session. This worked in W5 (GOOGL was revisited and bought). Drop a name after 5 sessions or if the thesis breaks.
- **Overreaction (>5% on specific news):** buy only after a green close that also **beats SPY by at least 1% that day**, or a full session holding above the prior low. A green day that comes from a broad rally isn't stock-specific confirmation (W5 GOOGL was bought on a midterm rally day, then fell with the sector).
- **No headline, no trade.** Skip big moves that no news explains.
- **Underreaction:** concrete material news with less than a 1% move. Log one named candidate **every session**. It was skipped on most W5 days, and it's the only way to make money in up weeks.
- Prefer single-company catalysts. Stay out while a sector or macro selloff keeps adding bad news. Don't buy into structural downtrends or real fundamental damage.

## Data hygiene
- If every price shows 0.0%, the data is stale: hold, and say so.

## Sizing and confidence
- Tournament results: confidence 1–4 avg −1.47% (n=6), 5–7 −5.49% (n=12), 8–10 −8.61% (n=2). Higher confidence has done worse every week so far.
- Starters use confidence 3–4. Never go above 6. Add only after a further confirmed close that beats SPY. Hold at most 2 positions.

## Exits
- **Time stop, applied strictly:** session 1 is the fill day. If the position is below cost at the end of session 3, exit at the next decision. Don't grant extensions; in W5 the trader gave GOOGL "one more session".
- **Thesis break:** if new negative news widens the story, exit or cut by half.
- **Take profit** once about half the overreaction has retraced.
