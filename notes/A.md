# Strategy A notes: Momentum

## Entry rules
- Buy names with positive 20-day AND 60-day returns that trade above their 50-day average.
- Rank by 60-day trend first and 20-day second. The 60d leader (PLTR) was the main pick in week 1. Keep doing this.
- Don't chase a single-day spike of more than 5%.
- If a name is more than 15% above its 50d average, treat it as extended: give it the smallest position or skip it.

## Sizing
- Hold 2-4 positions. Three positions was fine in week 1.
- Keep about $15-25 in cash so a rotation can happen without selling first.
- Use confidence 5-6 for ordinary momentum setups. Save 7+ for a name that leads on both 20d and 60d, is not extended, and has no spike. So far confidence has not predicted results (tournament: 1-4 avg +0.17%, 5-7 avg +0.13%, n=5), so don't raise it without a clear reason.

## Exits and rotation
- Rotate out when a holding falls below its 50d average, or when its 20d return turns negative and it drops out of the top momentum names.
- Hold a position at least 3 trading days unless it breaks its 50d average. Spread costs ($0.92 so far, about 0.18% of capital) are as large as our lead over SPY. Avoid churning.
- Make at most one rotation per day, and only when the replacement clearly ranks higher.

## Execution
- Place limit buys near the current ask, not far below it. All three fills came within about 0.3% of the limit.
- Check fills against the limit price. META was logged filling at 722.34 against a 720 limit, which should not happen with a buy limit. Report it if it happens again.
