# Strategy E: Claude's pick: Large-cap momentum, fully invested, low turnover

## Approach
Hold 3-4 liquid large-cap momentum leaders, or sector ETFs, that are in rising trends. To beat SPY, the money has to be invested. Sitting in cash is a guaranteed loss against SPY in an up week. Trade rarely, but get the orders filled.

## Universe
- US stocks over $10B market cap with average daily volume over $50M, or liquid ETFs (QQQ, XLK, SMH, XLF, XLI).
- Ignore small caps and penny stocks. Skip anything up more than 15% in 3 days.

## Entry
- Pick from the momentum-leaders list. Price must be above the 50-day average, and the 50-day must be above the 200-day.
- **Fills matter more than a few cents.** Set the limit at the last price or up to 0.3% above it. Don't bid under the market on a stock that's rising. If an order expires, re-place it at the last price at the next check-in. Don't chase more than one expiry.
- Prefer flat or pullback days, but don't wait more than one check-in for a pullback.
- Don't open a position within 2 days of earnings.
- If SPY is below its 50-day average or there's a broad selloff, hold at most 2 positions plus cash.

## Sizing
- Target: 3-4 positions with total cash at 10% or less by the end of the first check-in each week.
- Confidence 8 or higher gets a full slot (25-33%), 6-7 gets about two-thirds, 5 or lower means skip.
- **Cash sweep:** if more than 20% is still in cash after the stock picks (for example, because two-thirds slots leave a gap), put the remainder in QQQ or XLK as a core holding if allowed. Sell the sweep to fund a new leader.
- No more than 2 single stocks in the same sector (the sweep ETF doesn't count).

## Exits
- Sell if a position closes 8% below entry or below its 50-day average.
- Rotate only if a name falls off the leaders list AND a clearly stronger candidate exists. At most one rotation per slot per week.
- Trim half if a position is up 20% or more within 2 weeks.
- Ignore single red days or headlines that don't change the fundamentals.

- Default after the book is built: do nothing. Aim for 2-6 trades per week.
